About MagnetoClip

Capture the Web. MagnetoClip is an advanced download manager for Windows that turns the chaos of web downloads into an organized, one-click experience. Files, videos, music, documents, and torrents — MagnetoClip detects them as you browse, captures them reliably, and files them into smart, auto-sorted categories.

What is MagnetoClip?

Three Things It Does Especially Well

It Finds Downloadable Content

A companion browser extension watches the pages you visit and flags downloadable files, video and audio streams, and media you may not have noticed.

It Downloads Anything, Reliably

Whether it is a single file, a segmented multi-connection download, a live HLS/DASH stream, or a torrent, MagnetoClip fetches it with parallel connections, resumes interrupted transfers, and verifies finished files.

It Keeps You Organized

Every finished download is auto-sorted into a category — Image, Video, Audio, Document, Compressed, App/Software, Torrent, or Other — so your download folder stays tidy without any effort.

Its tagline, "Capture the Web", sums up the idea in three words: whatever is on the web, MagnetoClip can bring it home.

What's Inside

Built to Handle Everything You Download

Downloads That Just Work

Multi-connection engine (up to 64 connections), resume & retry with .mclip sidecar files, integrity checking before completion, global and per-download speed caps, pause with auto-resume, and a built-in speed test that measures download, upload, and latency.

Browser Companion Extension

Works with Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Vivaldi, and Chromium. Detects files and streamed media as you browse, resolves blob: URLs, captures streaming media with bundled FFmpeg, and can become your default downloader.

Full Torrent Manager

Magnet links and .torrent files, DHT/PEX and protocol encryption, sequential download for near-instant preview playback, optional auto-seed mode, and live status with a detailed file list.

Duplicate Detection

MagnetoClip indexes finished files and warns you before you download the same thing twice.

File Preview & Archiver

Preview images, video, audio, PDFs, text, and torrents right from the Downloads page, plus a built-in tool for archiving web content.

Remote Dashboard & More

Watch and control downloads from your phone or another computer on your local network (protected by a pairing token), route downloads through http, https, or socks5 proxies, and use the system tray for instant control.

Local-First by Design

Built with Privacy in Mind

MagnetoClip is designed around one conviction — your files and history belong to you.

  • Your download history, analytics, and settings live in a local database on your own computer.
  • No telemetry. Nothing about what you download is uploaded anywhere.
  • Your serial key and proxy passwords are stored in the Windows Credential Manager, never in plain files.
  • The Analytics dashboard is computed locally from your own history.
