Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about MagnetoClip licensing, compatibility, and features.

How does the license key activation work?
When you purchase a license, you receive a serial key in the format MGCL-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX. Simply paste it into MagnetoClip's Settings > License dialog. The key is cryptographically signed with Ed25519 and securely bound to your PC using the Windows Credential Manager keyring.
Can I transfer my license to a new PC?
Yes! MagnetoClip features self-service deactivation right inside the desktop application. Deactivating your current PC immediately frees your activation slot on the license server so you can activate your new machine.
Which browsers are supported by the companion extension?
Our Manifest V3 companion extension supports Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, Brave, Vivaldi, and Chromium browsers via high-speed Native Messaging.
Is my browsing and download history private?
100% private. MagnetoClip computes all download statistics, categories, and speed analytics locally on your machine. No telemetry or download URLs are ever sent to our servers.
